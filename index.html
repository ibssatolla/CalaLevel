                    Using CalaLevel

                    CalaLevel is a training log with game elements. It is not medical advice. Train within your limits and talk to a doctor if you are unsure about an exercise.

                    Your account

                    You are responsible for your password and for what happens under your account. Give correct information. One person, one account.

                    Your progress

                    XP, levels and rewards are calculated by the service. Attempts to change them by other means can lead to a reset or removal of the account.

                    Ending your account

                    You can delete your account in Profile, Account. Deleting removes your data from the service.

                    Privacy policy

                    Draft text, version . It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before a public launch.

                    What is stored

                    Your email address, your character (name and looks), your goals and training preferences, your workouts, quests, XP history and unlocks. Nothing else is collected by the app.

                    Where it is stored

                    In your Supabase project, protected by row level security so that only you can read your rows. A copy is kept in this browser so the app works offline.

                    Passwords

                    Passwords go straight to the authentication service. The app never stores them.

                    Demo mode

                    Demo mode uses sample data in this browser only and sends nothing to the service.

                    Deleting your data

                    Delete your account in Profile, Account. Your rows and your login are removed.

                    Progress found on this device

                    This browser has progress from before accounts existed.

                    You can add it to your new account once. It is never deleted from this device, and it never overwrites anything.

                    If you start fresh, the old data stays on this device. You can import it later from Profile, Account, as long as your account is still empty.
